About the owner

I am Rahul, and this is my father's shop.

My uncle Suresh started a tiny cloth counter on Main Market Road in 2012, and I joined him straight after college. I still remember folding shirts at 6 AM before the shutters went up.

Today we are a full clothing store, but the rule is the same: we show you the cloth, tell you the honest price, and fix the fit if it is off. Many of our customers now bring their children, and some bring their grandchildren.

Come in, sit down, have a cup of tea. You are never rushed here.

  • Fixed, clearly tagged prices. No bargaining games.
  • Free basic alterations on every purchase.
  • Easy 7-day exchange with the bill.
  • Same-day WhatsApp replies during shop hours.
  • Delivery across India for out-of-town family.
